# Demo Corp — Work From Home (WFH) Guidelines

**Document type:** HR Policy  
**Effective date:** 1 March 2026  
**Applies to:** All employees except roles marked as “site-only”

## 1. Hybrid model

Demo Corp follows a **hybrid** model:

- Employees work from the office **3 days per week** (Tue, Wed, Thu by default).
- Employees may work from home on **Monday and Friday**, unless a client meeting or sprint review requires office presence.

## 2. Eligibility

- Permanent employees who have completed **probation (90 days)** may use WFH.
- Interns and contractors need written approval from their manager for any WFH day.

## 3. Core hours

While on WFH, employees must be online during **core hours: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM** local time (IST).

Slack and email response expectation during core hours: **within 1 hour**.

## 4. Equipment

- Demo Corp provides a laptop. Home internet must be at least **50 Mbps** download.
- VPN must be connected when accessing internal systems.
- Recording meetings without consent is prohibited.

## 5. Client visits

If you have an on-site client visit, that day counts as an **office day**, even if it falls on a usual WFH weekday.

## 6. Exceptions

Managers may approve temporary full WFH for medical reasons for up to **14 days**, with HR notification.
