# Remote Work Equipment Policy

## 1. Eligible equipment
Employees on approved remote or hybrid schedules may request:
- Laptop stand and external monitor (up to $150 combined)
- Ergonomic keyboard and mouse (up to $80 combined)
- Noise-cancelling headset (up to $120)

## 2. Reimbursement process
Submit receipts within 30 days of purchase via the HRMS expense portal.
Reimbursement is processed within 10 business days of approval.

## 3. Equipment return
All company-purchased equipment must be returned within 14 days of
termination or role change to a non-remote position.
