# Demo Corp — Leave & Attendance Policy

**Document type:** HR Policy  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Applies to:** All full-time employees of Demo Corp

## 1. Annual leave

- Full-time employees receive **20 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave is credited on **1 January** each year (not monthly pro-rata for full-timers).
- Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of **5 days** into the next year.
- Carry-forward days expire on **31 March** of the following year if unused.

## 2. Sick leave

- Employees receive **12 days** of paid sick leave per year.
- Absences of more than **2 consecutive days** require a medical certificate.
- Sick leave does not carry forward to the next year.

## 3. Casual leave

- Employees receive **6 days** of casual leave per year for personal emergencies.
- Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave for a continuous absence longer than 10 days without manager approval.

## 4. How to apply

1. Submit a leave request in the HRMS portal at least **3 working days** before the start date (except sick leave).
2. Your reporting manager must approve the request.
3. HR will confirm the balance after approval.

## 5. Public holidays

Demo Corp observes **12 national/public holidays** each year. The holiday list is published by HR every December for the next year.

## 6. Contact

For leave balance questions, email **hr@example.com** or ask your reporting manager.
